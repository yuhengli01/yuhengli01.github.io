Research Interests

My research focuses on building agents that are capable, efficient, and reliable for complex real-world decision-making (e.g., multi-step planning under hard constraints, adversarial settings), using large language models (including diffusion language models) and reinforcement learning — I care not only about whether a model performs well, but about why it works and whether we can trust it in practice.

Current Focus
  • Diffusion language models, especially arbitrary-order generation and token embedding
  • RL post-training for diffusion / semi-autoregressive language models
  • Evaluating LLM agents with verifiable environments, test-time search, and verifier feedback (ongoing)
  • Multi-agent and game RL for decision-making under more real and complex conditions like robotics/game
Questions I'm Currently Exploring
  • What actually makes test-time search and verifier feedback work for LLM agents?
  • How should a diffusion language model decide the order in which to generate tokens?
