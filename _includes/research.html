Research

My research focuses on building learning systems that are capable, efficient, and reliable for complex real-world decision-making (e.g., adversarial attacks, unknown environments), using multi-agent reinforcement learning and diffusion models — I care not only about whether a model performs well, but about why it works and whether we can trust it in practice.

Current Research Interests
  • Diffusion language models, especially arbitrary-order generation and token embedding
  • RL post-training for diffusion / semi-autoregressive language models
  • Develop/Integrate vision-language models (VLMs) for complex decision-making problems such as route planning, to improve performance and reliability
  • Multi-agent and game RL for decision-making under more real and complex conditions like robotics/game
