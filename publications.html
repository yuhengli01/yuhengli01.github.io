Can Reinforcement Learning Solve Asymmetric Combinatorial-Continuous Zero-Sum Games?
Yuheng Li, Panpan Wang, Haipeng Chen
ICLR 2025 (Poster)
paper openreview code
RL Optimization GameTheory
Abstract

We define and study ACCES — asymmetric combinatorial–continuous zero-sum games — and prove the existence of a mixed Nash equilibrium by approximating with finite games. We propose two algorithms, CCDO and CCDORL, with convergence and computational analysis, and show over 5% average robustness gains across three ACCES instances.
